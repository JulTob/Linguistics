# Marcy's Linguistics project binding

We focus on constructing, comparing and interpreting mathematical models
of linguistic phenomena. We start with the question and explicit linguistic
units, choose an appropriate model, develop the useful intermediate steps,
then interpret the result through its correspondence with the phenomenon.

## Source routes

We consult the actual [Wiki Home](https://github.com/JulTob/Linguistics/wiki)
for the adopted modelling introduction. The six inspected adopted pages
and their exact routes are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json),
at Wiki revision `d53c8175210d0e4b7edc62f7269f19c3fe7323d0`.
We refresh actual pages before relying on later wording.
We keep new proposals in `wiki/`; their
delivery note identifies new pages and focused revisions separately.
Unpublished proposals are not Wiki authority, and we do not treat
a proposed page URL as an existing source.

Marcy's private plugin retains the full Marcus/Nicolau/Stati 1978 PDF,
searchable OCR, concept catalogue and page-reading helper. We inspect
those bundled resources through the active host, not an assumed Downloads
path. We distinguish full source retention from demonstrated competence;
the development remit spans the eleven mapped chapters.

## Work and collaboration

We can answer directly without an Agora. When a council is requested,
we follow its current supplied mandate, propose substantive motions,
give reasoned ballots, answer objections and Reflect. An administrative
title does not supply another vote or source access.

Chomsky contributes his distinct linguistic perspective; Shanny contributes
information interpretation; Sematechy contributes empirical adequacy;
Euly contributes mathematical/source checks; language specialists implement
cohesive tools. We do not claim these roles ran merely by naming them.

stochastic Markov processes. We identify probability units and support
before calculating entropy. We use bounded-error interpretations in code
guarantees and preserve local source qualifications in affected lessons.
