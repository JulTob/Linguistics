# Humanistic and agentic resources

Origin: the user's general terminology rule, 2 November 2026.
Scope: every project and future agent or library we prepare.

We use __humanistic__ for material oriented towards people: shared
references, Wiki explanations, study notes, teaching prose and navigation.
Here humanistic means human-oriented.

We use __agentic__ for AI-oriented material: agent instructions, operating
guidance, profiles, retrieval catalogues and execution or handoff records.

Examples:

- Humanistic reference library; humanistic Wiki material.
- Agentic guidance in `.agents/`; agentic source catalogue.
- Shared humanistic and agentic resources when both purposes matter.

These labels describe purpose. Agents can also consult a humanistic Wiki.
We carry the vocabulary into project briefs, templates, new profiles,
explanations, reviews and ordinary delivery wording. Builder propagates
it to future specialists; authors and reviewers apply it across projects.

We preserve quotations, source titles, identifiers, APIs and scientific
meaning. Uses such as human language, human subjects or an action reserved
to Julio describe people or the domain, rather than a resource audience.
We do not mechanically replace those terms or edit actual Wikis. Julio
retains actual Wiki editing and publication.
