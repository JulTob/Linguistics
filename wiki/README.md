## Why these additions are useful

The first model currently refers to a corpus without displaying its three
sentences. Its recursion discussion also refers to a colleague's claim
without stating the full grammar being tested. The proposed revision adds
the corpus and the rules, plus headings and a source-footer heading.
It preserves the existing equations, calculations and conclusions.

The second model currently opens with “the new assumptions” and refers
to “Your independence assumption”. The proposed revision states the
corpus, links the first model and makes the independence premise explicit
in shared we-language. It adds headings and preserves the calculations.

The coding lesson gives the existing `topics/coding/block_codes.py`
resource its own reusable explanation and usage example. The second model
already contains one coding example; the new page supplies the general
definitions, detection/correction distinction and a direct route to the
helper. Its source qualification stays beside the affected claim.

The Home insertion provides a reading route using the actual numbered
titles. The earlier optional navigation fragment used unnumbered routes
and was not incorporated; this proposal supersedes that fragment.

## Scope and subsequent work

This is the initial mathematical-linguistics collection, not an attempt
to turn the whole book into Wiki chapters at once. The other mapped
chapters can grow through useful questions and reviewed explanations.
The retained book stays in Marcy's private resources; it is not uploaded
to this public repository. Agentic source routes and project instructions
remain in `.agents/`.

Once a proposal is adopted, we refresh its actual Wiki route before
treating it as a source. Later Wiki edits take precedence over the
inspected revision and these proposal files.
